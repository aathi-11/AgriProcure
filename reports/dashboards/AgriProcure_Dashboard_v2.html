Model Insights
Flat class is hardest. Recall ≈ 0.35–0.43 while rise and fall are caught far more reliably (recall 0.68–0.77).
Tuned XGBoost / CatBoost show the highest hold-out accuracy but lower macro-F1 than logistic regression — they lean toward the majority class.
Fold-to-fold variance is as large as model-to-model gaps. Rankings are indicative only with 53 weeks of data.
Deep RNN / LSTM underperform tree models here — insufficient sequential data to learn complex temporal patterns from scratch.

🏭 Food Processor Procurement Playbook

1. Multi-Signal Forward Pre-Procurement
When SARIMA forecasts an upward trajectory AND Rule RULE_7506 fires (Heavy Rain Lag1 + Low Arrivals >15%), advance purchasing by 10–14 days captures 8–12% margin on raw material costs.

2. Geographic Sourcing Arbitrage
When heavy monsoon rain impacts western mandis (Pune/Nashik), shift spot procurement to southern hubs (Bangalore/Kolar) before cross-state price transmission — typically 48–72 hr lag.

3. Commodity-Specific Buffer Strategy
Tomato: Restrict buffer to 3–5 days under temperature-controlled transit.
Onion: Lock in forward contracts at seasonal troughs (April–May harvest glut).
Potato: Contract 45-day supply from cold-storage hubs (Agra) during quarterly troughs.

🌾 FPO & Farmer Selling Playbook

1. Dynamic Withholding Strategy
When rainfall disrupts regional transport and arrivals drop >20%, FPOs with on-farm staging should delay dispatch by 48–72 hr. Association rules show 76.6% certainty of a price spike.

2. Glut Liquidation Protocol
If arrivals surge >20% and SARIMA indicates a downward trajectory, liquidate immediately on spot markets. Do not hold perishable stock into a decelerating market.

3. Shared Logistics Coordination
Combine FPO dispatches to tier-1 mandis (Ahmedabad/Bangalore) where liquidity absorbs supply surges without drastic localized price crashes.
